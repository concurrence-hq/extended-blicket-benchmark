## 3. Activation-Count Decomposition

The hidden moderator switches the rule after the 3rd successful detector activation. Every episode falls into one of three categories:

- **Pre-switch** (activations < 3): The rule never changed. The true rule remains {A, B}. These are "freebies."
- **Exactly-N** (activations = 3): The rule switched on the agent's final activation. The agent triggered the switch but submitted before observing any post-switch evidence. Accuracy is 0% by structural necessity — this is not a reasoning failure but a timing trap.
- **Reasoning-eligible** (activations > 3): The rule switched and the agent observed post-switch evidence before submitting. This is the only category where reasoning quality can be assessed.

|------|-------|:----------:|:---------:|:------------------:|:-----------:|
| A | base_llm (75) | 13/13 (100%) | 0/7 (0%) | **22/30 (73.3%)** | 22/37 (59.5%) |
| B | cg_only (75) | 3/3 (100%) | 0/14 (0%) | **31/33 (93.9%)** | 31/47 (66.0%) |
| C | full_cg_db (75) | 4/4 (100%) | 0/3 (0%) | **41/43 (95.3%)** | 41/46 (89.1%) |

Pre-switch episodes are 100% correct (except Cell E's single pre-switch episode, a parse failure at 9 steps). Exactly-N episodes are universally 0% correct across all cells and all agents — consistent with all prior runs. The exactly-N category is structurally unsolvable.

**Key findings:**

1. **Both scaffolded agents significantly outperform the bare LLM on reasoning-eligible accuracy.** CG-only: +20.6pp (p=0.038); full_cg_db: +22.0pp (p=0.013).

2. **CG-only and full_cg_db do not differ on reasoning-eligible accuracy** (93.9% vs 95.3%, p=1.000). When the agent has post-switch evidence to reason about, the CG alone provides nearly all of the reasoning quality improvement. DB adds negligible additional accuracy.

3. **The post-switch accuracy gap (59.5% vs 89.1%, p=0.002) conflates two separable mechanisms.** It combines reasoning quality (where CG is the primary driver) with exactly-N avoidance (where DB is the primary driver). Decomposing these mechanisms requires the three-way classification presented below.

### 3.4 Exactly-N Rate Analysis

The three agents differ dramatically in how many post-switch episodes fall into the exactly-N structural trap:

| Cell | Agent | Switch Rate | Exactly-N Count | Exactly-N Rate (of post-switch) |
|------|-------|:-----------:|:---------------:|:-------------------------------:|
| A | base_llm | 37/50 (74%) | 7 | 18.9% |
| B | cg_only | 47/50 (94%) | **14** | **29.8%** |
| C | full_cg_db | 46/50 (92%) | 3 | **6.5%** |
| D | base_llm | 20/25 (80%) | 5 | 25.0% |
| E | full_cg_db | 24/25 (96%) | 4 | 16.7% |

CG-only (Cell B) has the **highest** exactly-N rate (29.8%) — nearly 5× that of full_cg_db (6.5%). CG drives deeper exploration (94% switch rate vs 74% for base_llm), triggering the rule change in almost every episode. But without DB's explicit rule-change notification, the CG agent often submits its current hypothesis immediately after the switch, before exploring further. DB's "[SYSTEM] The causal rule appears to have changed" message prevents this premature submission.

**This is the key asymmetry:** CG makes the agent more likely to trigger the switch (beneficial), but also more likely to fall into the exactly-N trap (harmful). DB prevents the trap without affecting reasoning quality. The two mechanisms operate on different failure modes.

## 4. Three-Way Agent Comparison (Primary Question)

**Question:** Does `full_cg_db`'s advantage come from CG structure or from DB specifically?

**Answer:** CG and DB contribute through **completely separable pathways** — CG improves reasoning quality, DB prevents structural trap failures.

### 4.1 Pathway Decomposition

| Pathway | Mechanism | Evidence | Primary Driver |
|---------|-----------|----------|----------------|
| **Reasoning quality** | Given post-switch evidence, how accurately does the agent identify {C,D,E}? | RE accuracy: base_llm 73.3% → cg_only 93.9% → full_cg_db 95.3% | **CG** (94% of +22.0pp gain) |
| **Exactly-N avoidance** | How often does the agent avoid the structural trap? | Exactly-N rate: cg_only 29.8% → full_cg_db 6.5% | **DB** (system message prevents premature submission) |

### 4.2 Reasoning Quality Attribution

| Comparison | RE Accuracy | Delta | Share of 22.0pp gap | p (Fisher's) |
|------------|:----------:|:-----:|:-------------------:|:---:|
| A (base_llm) → B (cg_only) | 73.3% → 93.9% | **+20.6pp** | **94%** | 0.038 |
| B (cg_only) → C (full_cg_db) | 93.9% → 95.3% | **+1.4pp** | **6%** | 1.000 |

CG alone accounts for 94% of the reasoning quality improvement. The CG's structured exploration — systematic object combination testing, state tracking — enables the agent to isolate {C, D, E} from the five-object space far more reliably than the bare LLM's undirected search. DB adds a statistically negligible +1.4pp.

### 4.3 Exactly-N Avoidance Attribution

| Agent | Exactly-N Rate (of post-switch) | Mechanism |
|-------|:-------------------------------:|-----------|
| base_llm | 18.9% (7/37) | No CG, no DB — submits when confident |
| cg_only | **29.8% (14/47)** | CG drives deep exploration → more switches, but no revision cue → submits pre-switch hypothesis |
| full_cg_db | **6.5% (3/46)** | DB system message interrupts premature submission → agent re-explores |

CG *worsens* the exactly-N rate (from 18.9% to 29.8%) because it drives the agent into the switch zone without providing a revision mechanism. DB dramatically *improves* it (from 29.8% to 6.5%) by injecting an explicit notification that the rule has changed.

### 4.4 Why Post-Switch Accuracy Is Misleading

The post-switch accuracy gap (A: 59.5% vs C: 89.1%) conflates both pathways. Decomposing:

| Factor | Contribution to 29.6pp post-switch gap | Notes |
|--------|:---------------------------------------:|-------|
| CG reasoning quality improvement | +20.6pp × (proportion of RE episodes) | Applies only to reasoning-eligible episodes |
| DB exactly-N avoidance | Removes ~10 structural failures from Cell C | Converts 0%-accuracy episodes into RE episodes |
| Interaction (more RE episodes with higher accuracy) | Remainder | CG creates more RE opportunities, which DB preserves |

The original report's "78% DB, 22% CG" attribution (computed on post-switch accuracy) was incorrect because it did not separate reasoning quality from structural trap avoidance. When properly decomposed, CG drives reasoning quality and DB drives trap avoidance — complementary, not substitutive.

### 6.1 System Message and Exactly-N (Cell C)

The DB system message fires when the trigger model detects anomalous observations. In Cell C:

All 43 reasoning-eligible episodes in Cell C received the DB system message. The 2 "no system message" failures from the original analysis (which had 0% accuracy) were exactly-N episodes where the agent submitted before DB fired — not a DB failure mode, but a timing artifact.

### 6.2 DB System Message Effectiveness (RE Episodes Only)

| Condition | Episodes | Correct | Accuracy |
|-----------|:--------:|:-------:|:--------:|
| RE episodes, DB system message received (Cell C) | 43 | 41 | **95.3%** |
| RE episodes, no system message (Cell C) | 0 | — | — |
| Pre-switch (rule never changed) | 4 | 4 | 100.0% |

Among RE episodes, DB system message coverage is 100% in Cell C. The system message does not independently improve RE accuracy (Cell B achieves 93.9% without any DB) but prevents exactly-N failures by triggering re-exploration before submission.

### 6.3 MODERATOR_DETECTION CG State

Among Cell C post-switch episodes:
- 22/46 entered MODERATOR_DETECTION state: 90.9% post-switch accuracy
- 24/46 did not enter MODERATOR_DETECTION: 87.5% post-switch accuracy

Among Cell C RE episodes only:
- MODERATOR_DETECTION reached in ~20/43 RE episodes
- Accuracy similar whether or not the state was reached

The MODERATOR_DETECTION CG state provides minimal additional benefit over the system message alone, consistent with the finding that CG structure (not DB notification) drives reasoning quality.

### 6.4 DB's Functional Role: Exactly-N Prevention

The DB mechanism's primary contribution on {C,D,E} is not improving reasoning accuracy but preventing premature submission:

1. CG agent triggers rule switch (94% switch rate)
2. Without DB: agent often submits immediately → exactly-N trap (29.8% rate for cg_only)
3. With DB: system message interrupts → agent re-explores → exactly-N trap avoided (6.5% rate)

This reframes DB's value from "reasoning quality enhancer" to "exploration continuation mechanism" — it keeps the agent investigating when the environment changes, ensuring the agent reaches the reasoning-eligible zone where CG's structured exploration can operate.

## 10. Decision Criteria Assessment

### 10.1 Pre-Registered Questions

**Q1: Does full_cg_db's advantage over base_llm on {C,D,E} replicate at n=50?**
Yes. RE accuracy: 95.3% vs 73.3%, p=0.013. Post-switch accuracy: 89.1% vs 59.5%, p=0.002. Both significant.

**Q2: Is the advantage structural (CG) or mechanism-specific (DB)?**
Both — but through separable pathways. **CG drives reasoning quality** (73.3% → 93.9% RE accuracy, +20.6pp, p=0.038). **DB drives exactly-N avoidance** (29.8% → 6.5% exactly-N rate). On reasoning-eligible accuracy alone, cg_only and full_cg_db are indistinguishable (93.9% vs 95.3%, p=1.000).

**Q3: Does runway compression matter?**
For reasoning-eligible accuracy: No significant effect for either agent (p>0.5). For raw/post-switch accuracy: full_cg_db drops 18pp (90% → 72%) due to increased exactly-N rate under compressed runway, while base_llm is unaffected.

### 10.2 New Finding: CG Drives Reasoning, DB Prevents Structural Traps

The three-way classification reveals that CG and DB contribute through completely different failure-mode pathways:

- **CG improves reasoning quality.** Among episodes with post-switch evidence, the CG's structured exploration enables near-perfect isolation of {C,D,E} (93.9–95.3% RE accuracy vs 73.3% for the bare LLM). This is statistically significant (p=0.038).

- **DB prevents premature submission.** CG-only has the highest exactly-N rate in the run (29.8% of post-switch episodes) because deep CG-driven exploration reliably triggers the switch, but without an explicit notification, the agent submits its pre-switch hypothesis immediately after. DB's system message interrupts this, reducing the exactly-N rate to 6.5%.

- **Neither component alone achieves the full effect.** CG-only has excellent reasoning (93.9%) but poor exactly-N avoidance (29.8%). Full_cg_db has excellent reasoning (95.3%) and excellent exactly-N avoidance (6.5%). The architecture needs both components because they address orthogonal failure modes.

## 11. Conclusions

### 11.1 Summary of Findings

1. **CG is the primary driver of reasoning quality** (94% of the +22.0pp RE accuracy gain over base_llm). On reasoning-eligible episodes, cg_only achieves 93.9% accuracy — nearly matching full_cg_db's 95.3% and far exceeding base_llm's 73.3%. This is statistically significant (p=0.038).

2. **DB is the primary driver of exactly-N avoidance.** CG-only has the worst exactly-N rate (29.8% of post-switch episodes) because it reliably triggers the switch but lacks a revision cue. DB reduces this to 6.5% by injecting an explicit rule-change notification.

3. **CG and DB address orthogonal failure modes.** CG improves what the agent does with post-switch evidence (reasoning quality). DB ensures the agent continues exploring after the switch rather than submitting prematurely (exactly-N avoidance). Neither substitutes for the other.

4. **The "CG rigidity paradox" does not exist.** The original report's claim that CG makes beliefs stickier was based on CG-only's 87.5% "stuck on {A,B}" error rate. In fact, all 14 "stuck on {A,B}" errors were exactly-N structural failures — episodes where the agent never observed post-switch evidence. Zero reasoning-eligible episodes across any cell answered {A, B}. CG-only's reasoning is excellent (93.9% RE accuracy); its problem is the exactly-N trap, not belief rigidity.

5. **Runway compression affects exactly-N rate, not reasoning quality.** RE accuracy shows no significant degradation at 50 steps for either agent. The raw accuracy drop for full_cg_db (90% → 72%) is driven by increased exactly-N rate under compressed runway (less time for DB to fire).

6. **Raw accuracy and post-switch accuracy are misleading** due to exactly-N contamination. Reasoning-eligible accuracy is the necessary primary metric for agent comparison on this task.

### 11.2 CID Thesis Implications

- **Composition provides separable, pathway-specific advantages.** CG and DB do not duplicate or substitute — they address completely different failure modes (reasoning quality vs structural trap avoidance). This is a strong form of the composition thesis: the whole exceeds the sum because the parts operate on orthogonal axes.

- **Adding structure can create new failure modes that require additional structure to address.** CG drives deeper exploration (good) but increases exactly-N exposure (bad). DB resolves the exactly-N problem that CG creates. The architecture needs both because each component's benefit depends on the other's presence.

- **External memory cues (DB) serve as exploration continuation mechanisms,** not reasoning enhancers. The system message's value is preventing premature submission, not improving the quality of post-switch inference. This suggests that DB's role in healthcare agents may be less about "better reasoning" and more about "knowing when to keep investigating."

- **Structured exploration (CG) provides genuine reasoning advantages on hard tasks.** The 20.6pp RE accuracy improvement from CG alone (p=0.038) confirms that structured exploration — systematic combination testing, state tracking — outperforms undirected LLM search when the combinatorial space is large (10 possible triples from 5 objects).

### 11.3 Recommended Next Steps

1. **Exactly-N mitigation.** Investigate mechanisms to reduce the exactly-N rate for CG agents: post-switch exploration mandates, submission delay after switch detection, or adaptive switch thresholds.
2. **CG exploration policy.** The CG's 93.9% RE accuracy suggests that structured exploration alone is powerful. Test whether CG modifications (e.g., mandatory retest-after-switch) can reduce the exactly-N rate without DB.
3. **Runway optimization.** Test intermediate max_steps values (55, 60, 65) to find the minimum runway at which DB's system message reliably fires before submission.
4. **DB failure analysis.** In Cell C, 2/3 exactly-N episodes received no system message. Investigate whether earlier DB firing (lower threshold) could catch these cases.

### 11.4 Correction Notice

This report corrects the original report (also dated 2026-02-26) which:
- Reported post-switch accuracy as the primary metric, conflating exactly-N structural failures with reasoning-eligible accuracy
- Attributed 78% of the advantage to DB and 22% to CG (inverted; correct attribution: CG drives 94% of reasoning quality, DB drives exactly-N avoidance)
- Claimed a "CG rigidity paradox" based on exactly-N contamination in error classification (all 33 "stuck on {A,B}" errors were exactly-N structural failures, not reasoning failures)
- Omitted statistical tests (Fisher's exact, Wilson CIs)
- Omitted the three-way episode classification (pre-switch / exactly-N / reasoning-eligible)

## Appendix C: Three-Way Analysis Verification

The three-way classification was computed from raw trace files by extracting `env_info.successful_activations` and `env_info.rule_switched` from all 200 episodes. Classification rules:
- Pre-switch: `rule_switched == false`
- Exactly-N: `rule_switched == true` AND `successful_activations == 3`
- Reasoning-eligible: `rule_switched == true` AND `successful_activations > 3`

Verification against error data confirmed a perfect 1:1 mapping: all 33 "stuck on {A,B}" errors have `successful_activations == 3` (exactly-N), and all reasoning-eligible errors have `successful_activations > 3`. No exceptions.

Statistical tests: Fisher's exact (two-sided) via `scipy.stats.fisher_exact`. Confidence intervals: Wilson score method.
