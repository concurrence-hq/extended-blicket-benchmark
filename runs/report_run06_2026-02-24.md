## 1. Overview

Run 06 is the primary experiment testing whether compositional architecture enables coordinate system revision through deeper exploration and mechanistic detection. It uses two switch points (sw=3, sw=5) across all three agent types, producing the largest single-run dataset in this benchmark series.

### 1.1 Design

| Cell | Agent | Switch N | Episodes | Purpose |
|------|-------|:--------:|:--------:|---------|
| A1 | base_llm | 3 | 50 | Shared challenge: encounters switch by exploration-range coincidence |
| A2 | cg_only | 3 | 50 | Shared challenge: CG explores deeply, no DB rescue |
| A3 | full_cg_db | 3 | 50 | Shared challenge: CG explores deeply, DB detects and rescues |
| B1 | base_llm | 5 | 50 | CG-selective control: expected never to reach threshold |
| B2 | cg_only | 5 | 50 | CG-selective challenge: CG explores past threshold, no DB |
| B3 | full_cg_db | 5 | 50 | CG-selective challenge: CG explores past threshold, DB available |

### 4.3 The sw=5 Reversal

The planning prompt predicted full_cg_db > cg_only at sw=5 (H2). The result is reversed: **cg_only 90% > full_cg_db 84%**. This is explained entirely by differential switch rates:

| Agent | Switch Rate | Post-Switch Accuracy | Not-Switched Accuracy |
|-------|:-----------:|:--------------------:|:---------------------:|
| cg_only | 18% (9/50) | 44.4% (4/9) | 100% (41/41) |
| full_cg_db | 54% (27/50) | 70.4% (19/27) | 100% (23/23) |

full_cg_db has *better* post-switch accuracy (70.4% vs 44.4%) but triggers the switch nearly 3x more often (54% vs 18%), creating far more episodes at risk. The DB rescues many of these episodes — but not all, and the sheer volume of switched episodes drives overall accuracy down. cg_only benefits from its lower switch rate: fewer episodes encounter the rule change in the first place.

This finding clarifies the architecture's value proposition: the DB mechanism works (70.4% vs 44.4% among switched episodes), but at sw=5, the CG+DB combination drives exploration so deep that it creates more vulnerability than the DB can fully rescue.

### 6.2 H1 Assessment (Exploration Depth)

**H1 predicted:** CG-driven agents achieve more activations than base_llm, determining switch encounter rates.

**Result: Partially confirmed, with a surprise.** full_cg_db consistently explores deepest (mean 7.3 at sw=3, 6.1 at sw=5) and has the highest switch rates (98%, 54%). The separation between agents is clearest at sw=5, where full_cg_db's 54% switch rate dramatically exceeds base_llm's 6% and cg_only's 18%.

### 6.3 Exploration Ceiling Revision

The Run 5 pilot reported base_llm never exceeds 4 activations (max=4 across 45 episodes). Run 06 shows higher values: max=10 at sw=3, max=9 at sw=5. The exploration ceiling is not hard — it is a distributional property where most base_llm episodes explore shallowly (median 3.0 at sw=5) but a tail extends further. The 6% switch rate at sw=5 (vs 0% in the pilot) is consistent with a soft ceiling at n=15 pilot resolution.

## 8. Error Analysis

### 8.1 Activation-Count Decomposition: The Universal Failure Mode

Every episode can be classified into exactly one of three categories based on the number of successful activations accumulated before submission:

**sw=3 (n=50 per agent):**

| Category | base_llm | cg_only | full_cg_db |
|----------|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Pre-switch** (< 3 activations) | 9/9 (100%) | 8/8 (100%) | 1/1 (100%) |
| **Exactly 3** activations | 0/3 (0%) | 0/8 (0%) | 0/4 (0%) |
| **Reasoning-eligible** (> 3 activations) | 36/38 (94.7%) | 33/34 (97.1%) | 43/45 (95.6%) |
| **Overall** | 45/50 (90.0%) | 41/50 (82.0%) | 44/50 (88.0%) |

**sw=5 (n=50 per agent):**

| Category | base_llm | cg_only | full_cg_db |
|----------|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Pre-switch** (< 5 activations) | 47/47 (100%) | 41/41 (100%) | 23/23 (100%) |
| **Exactly 5** activations | 0 eps | 0/5 (0%) | 0/8 (0%) |
| **Reasoning-eligible** (> 5 activations) | 3/3 (100%) | 4/4 (100%) | 19/19 (100%) |
| **Overall** | 50/50 (100%) | 45/50 (90.0%) | 42/50 (84.0%) |

**Interpretation:**

- **Pre-switch** (activations < N): The rule never switched. Always 100% accurate — the agent correctly identifies the original conjunctive {A,B} rule.
- **Exactly N** activations: The agent triggers the switch on its Nth glow but submits immediately — reporting the now-obsolete pre-switch rule with zero post-switch exploration. Always 0% accurate. This is a structural property of the task, not an agent failure.
- **Reasoning-eligible** (activations > N): The agent explores past the switch point, encounters contradictory evidence, and has the opportunity to revise. At sw=5, reasoning-eligible accuracy is **100% for all agents**. At sw=3, it is 95-97% — the 5 late-switch failures are detailed in Section 8.3.

The headline accuracy differences are driven by two factors: (a) how many episodes land exactly on N activations (always 0% accuracy), and (b) at sw=5, whether the switch is triggered at all (base_llm triggers it only 6% of the time vs full_cg_db 54%). Among reasoning-eligible episodes, all agents perform comparably.

## 11. Conclusions and Implications

### 11.1 Hypothesis Assessment

| Hypothesis | Result |
|------------|--------|
| **H1** (Exploration Depth) | **Partially confirmed.** full_cg_db explores deepest (mean 7.3 at sw=3, 6.1 at sw=5). But base_llm explores deeper than expected (mean 5.7 at sw=3, max 10). Separation is clearest at sw=5. |
| **H2** (Architectural Rescue) | **Confirmed at sw=3, reversed at sw=5.** full_cg_db > cg_only post-switch accuracy at both switch points (87.8% vs 78.6% at sw=3; 70.4% vs 44.4% at sw=5), but overall accuracy at sw=5 is reversed because full_cg_db triggers the switch far more often. |
| **H3** (DB Mechanism) | **Confirmed.** PPV 97.7% (sw=3) and 90.9% (sw=5). Sensitivity 100% at both. RC firing is necessary and nearly sufficient for correct post-switch answers. |
| **H4** (CG-Selective Challenge) | **Confirmed with caveat.** base_llm achieves 100% at sw=5, but 3/50 episodes (6%) did trigger the switch — the ceiling is softer than the pilot suggested. |
| **H5** (CG Harm) | **Confirmed.** cg_only (82%) < base_llm (90%) at sw=3 in Run 6, consistent with Runs 3/3B. |
| **H6** (Runway Effect) | **Indeterminate.** switch_step is not recorded in traces, preventing direct runway calculation. However, short episodes with the switch triggered are consistently wrong. |

### 11.2 Key Findings

**Finding 1: full_cg_db robustly outperforms cg_only at sw=3.** Combined across 110 episodes per agent: 89.1% vs 77.3%, p=0.015, BF₁₀=100.4. This is replicated across all three independent batches. The DB mechanism provides a genuine advantage over the static CG.

**Finding 2: full_cg_db does NOT robustly outperform base_llm at sw=3.** Combined: 89.1% vs 87.3%, p=0.418. The accuracy advantage is ~2pp — too small for the sample size to detect.

**Finding 3: The sw=5 reversal reveals the depth-vulnerability tradeoff.** full_cg_db explores deepest (54% switch rate), which creates maximum exposure to the rule change. The DB rescues 70.4% of switched episodes (vs cg_only's 44.4%), but the volume of switched episodes is so high that overall accuracy falls below cg_only. The architecture's value is in *post-switch recovery*, not in *overall accuracy*.

**Finding 4: The "exactly N" failure mode is universal.** Every agent, at every switch point, fails 100% of the time when it achieves exactly N activations. Among late-switch episodes (> N activations), accuracy is 95-100% regardless of agent type. Agent-level accuracy differences are driven by the *distribution of exploration depth relative to the switch point*, not by differential causal reasoning ability.

**Finding 5: The RC mechanism is reliable and mechanistically transparent.** Sensitivity is 100% at both switch points. PPV ranges from 90.9% to 97.7%. The DB's rule_change_hypothesis behavior is the strongest, most consistent finding across all runs — it is a reliable, transparent detection mechanism for coordinate system change.

### 11.3 Applying Decision Rules (from Planning Prompt)

**full_cg_db vs cg_only significant at sw=3 (p=0.015) but NOT at sw=5 (reversed).**

Per the pre-specified decision framework: check whether the sw=5 null result is due to (a) insufficient switched episodes or (b) the DB creating more vulnerability than it can rescue. The answer is (b): full_cg_db has 27 switched episodes (adequate power), but its 54% switch rate — 3x higher than cg_only's 18% — generates more failures than the DB's 70.4% post-switch recovery rate can offset.

**full_cg_db vs base_llm remains non-significant (p=0.418).**

Per the decision framework: pivot the narrative to the mechanistic and exploration-depth findings. The accuracy comparison is underpowered for a ~2pp effect. The exploration ceiling, RC mechanism, and depth-vulnerability tradeoff are all stronger, more informative findings.
